How customers order

  1. Open a customer (Customers → name) and switch on Online shop with a password. Their mobile number is their login.
  2. Send them the shop link: {{ shop_url }}
  3. They pick products and place an order. It appears under Online orders with a red count.
  4. Call them to confirm, click Make bill, pack, deliver.
