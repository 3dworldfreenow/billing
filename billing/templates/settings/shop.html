Online shop

When it's closed (for example on the offline counter PC), customers can't log in and only the admin can browse the shop as a preview.

Google can then list your products. Visitors still need an approved login to place an order; they're asked to log in (or call you) at checkout.

Search engines find your products through {{ request.host_url }}sitemap.xml.

{{ icon('cart') }} Preview the shop

How customers order

  1. Open a customer (Customers → name) and switch on Online shop with a password. Their mobile number is their login.
  2. Send them the shop link: {{ shop_url }}
  3. They pick products and place an order. It appears under Online orders with a red count.
  4. Call them to confirm, click Make bill, pack, deliver.
